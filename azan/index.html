SiraatOne Azan

Prayer times and Azan on a large screen, for your home or masjid.

Get it on Google Play

Features

Prayer times

Use your device location, or type a city or ZIP/postal code. Location is optional.

Azan at prayer time

Choose your Azan sound, or pick your own audio file.

Iqama and Jumu'ah

Show Iqama and Jumu'ah times for your masjid.

Your display

Add your masjid name, logo and wallpaper. Keep Screen Awake is on by default.

Works in the background

Azan alarms are restored after a restart and kept up to date daily.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Settings stay on your device.

Phones

SiraatOne Azan is designed for TV and tablet. On a phone it shows a notice first; tap Open anyway to use the same layout in landscape. Azan alarms and sound work fully on phones.
