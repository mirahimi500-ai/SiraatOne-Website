SiraatOne Quran (Android, iOS and Windows)

In short: the app works fully without an account; signing in with Google or Apple is optional and only syncs your own data between your devices; no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries; your location is used only for prayer times and the Qibla and is never sent to us; without an account, your bookmarks, notes and settings stay on your device.

Information we collect

Without an account, SiraatOne does not collect personal information and does not send your data to the developer. If you choose to sign in, only the information described in "Optional account and sync" below is used. We never sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your phone, tablet or PC:

  • Settings, such as language, theme and reading options.
  • Bookmarks, favorites, highlights, personal notes and reading progress.
  • Tasbih counts and Islamic calendar fasting marks.
  • Prayer settings, your location or chosen city, and your Azan alert choices.
  • Content you download, such as Quran script packs, recitation audio, translations and Hadith.

This information is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Optional account and sync

The app works fully without an account. Without signing in, all your data stays on your device.

  • Who we are: SiraatOne is developed by Zaiba Technologies, LLC, Pennsylvania, USA. Contact: privacy@zaibatech.com.
  • What we receive when you sign in: if you choose Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that provider. Apple users may hide their email; Apple then gives a private relay address.
  • What syncs: bookmarks, favourites, highlights, personal notes, My Ibadah progress and app settings. They are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, United States), linked to your account and readable only by your account.
  • Purpose: only to sync your own data between your devices. No advertising. Your data is never sold and never shared.
  • Security: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and the sign-in token is kept in your device's secure storage.
  • Service providers: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In) and Apple (Sign in with Apple).
  • Deleting your account: in the app, Settings → Account & Sync → Delete Account deletes your account and all cloud data immediately. You can also email privacy@zaibatech.com. See Delete your SiraatOne account.

Zakat calculator

To show today's gold and silver prices, the app asks a free public exchange-rate service (delivered through jsDelivr / Cloudflare). Only the currency code (for example "USD") is sent — no personal data.

Backups

You can export a backup file and import it again. The file is saved only where you choose with your device's file picker. The app does not upload it anywhere.

Location

Prayer times and the Qibla use your location on this device. The app asks for permission first, and you can choose a city instead. Your location is not sent to us and is not used for tracking or advertising. City search, place names and time zones come from the built-in map services of your phone or tablet (Apple or Google), which receive the place or position being looked up.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to download Islamic content you ask for, such as Quran text, recitation audio, translations and Hadith. Like any internet request, the server you connect to receives your IP address and basic technical information about the request. These providers' own privacy policies apply. The app may connect to: Quran Foundation (quran.foundation, qurancdn.com, api.quran.com), HadeethEnc.com, EveryAyah.com, mp3quran.net, QuranicAudio.com, QuranFlash, GitHub and jsDelivr (hosted data files) and Hugging Face (hosted data files). Links to sources such as Sunnah.com open in your browser.

Text-to-speech

Pronunciation features use the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The app passes the text to that engine. The engine's own terms apply.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the text you chose is passed to the app you pick in your device's share menu. Nothing is shared without your action.

Permissions

Android: internet; location (optional — you can choose a city instead); notifications; exact alarms and start at boot, to schedule prayer-time alerts and restore them after a restart; and a foreground service for media playback, so recitation keeps playing.

iOS: location only while the app is open, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction — the app never tracks your location in the background; background audio for recitation; and background refresh.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or motion permissions. The Qibla uses the compass only: no image is taken, saved or sent.

Windows (Microsoft Store)

SiraatOne Quran for Windows is distributed through the Microsoft Store (publisher: SiraatOne; app updates come through the Store). Updated 27 November 2026.

  • Location: used only for prayer times and the Qibla, with your permission in the Windows location settings. To show the place name and for city search, the coordinates or the city you type are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim; nothing else is shared. Results are cached on your PC. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • OpenStreetMap also receives the app language and, as with any internet request, your IP address; the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy applies.
  • Time zone: found offline on your PC; no service is used.
  • Prayer and Azan notifications: scheduled locally by Windows on your PC. No server is involved.
  • Backups: saved only to a file you pick on your own PC.
  • Quran and Islamic content: downloaded from public content services only when you open or download it: Quran text and the Uthmanic font from Quran.com / the Quran Foundation (api.quran.com, verses.quran.foundation); recitation audio from audio.qurancdn.com, download.quranicaudio.com, everyayah.com and mp3quran.net; other content files from raw.githubusercontent.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net, huggingface.co, the-quran-project.github.io and hadeethenc.com; currency exchange rates (Zakat) from currency-api.pages.dev. These requests contain only what to download, no personal data; as with any internet request, the services see your PC's IP address.
  • Links (Store pages, sunnah.com, Quran Flash, licence pages) open in your browser only when you tap them.
  • No account, no analytics, no crash-report library, no advertising or tracking, and no data is sold.
  • Privacy questions: privacy@zaibatech.com. Support: support@zaibatech.com.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and works without an account. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13 (or the minimum age in their country). If you believe a child has signed in, email privacy@zaibatech.com and we will delete the account.

Deleting your data

Without an account, nothing is stored on our servers. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. On iPhone or iPad, delete the app. On Windows, uninstall the app in Settings → Apps.

If you signed in, delete your account in the app (Settings → Account & Sync → Delete Account) or follow the steps on the Delete account page. This deletes your account and all synced data; nothing is kept.

SiraatOne Azan (Android, TV and tablet)

In short: no account or sign-in; no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries; your location is used only to calculate prayer times; your settings stay on your device.

Information we collect

SiraatOne does not collect, sell or share personal information. The app has no user accounts and does not send your personal data to the developer.

Location

To calculate prayer times, the app needs your location. You can either:

  • allow the app to use your device location (optional; you can deny this permission), or
  • type a city or ZIP/postal code.

The coordinates are saved only on your device. To find the place and its time zone, the app sends the city/ZIP text or the coordinates to these services, whose own privacy policies apply:

  • Open-Meteo (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com and api.open-meteo.com): place search and time zone.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): the city name for your coordinates.
  • Zippopotam.us (api.zippopotam.us): US ZIP code lookup.

Information stored on your device

Prayer settings, location, Azan and alarm choices, Iqama and Jumu'ah times, display settings, profile name and logo, and any wallpaper or audio you choose. This information stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Notifications, alarms and background audio

The app uses notifications, exact alarms and a background audio service only to play the Azan and reminders at prayer times. Nothing is sent to us.

Files you choose

If you pick your own Azan audio, wallpaper or logo, the app reads that file from your device and keeps a copy on the device only.

Permissions

Internet, location (optional), notifications, exact alarms, start at boot (to restore alarms after a restart), wake lock and background audio playback. The app does not access contacts, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

No personal data is stored on our servers. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

SiraatOne Azan for Windows

Windows Azan app section updated: 27 November 2026 (Microsoft Store: SiraatOne Azan, 9PHZ87D2BW12). SiraatOne Quran for Windows is covered in the Quran app section.

In short: no account or sign-in; no advertising, analytics or crash reports; your location is used only to calculate prayer times; your settings stay on your PC.

Information we collect

SiraatOne does not collect, sell or share personal information. The app has no user accounts and does not send your personal data to the developer.

Location

To calculate prayer times, the app uses your PC's location, only if you allow it in Windows (Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Location). The app has no Qibla.

The coordinates are saved only on your PC. They are sent to these services, whose own privacy policies apply:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): to show the name of your area.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com): to find the time zone of your location.

As with any internet request, these services also see your PC's IP address. Nothing else is shared.

Information stored on your PC

Prayer settings, location, Azan choices and volume, Iqama and Jumu'ah times, and widget, screensaver and display settings. This information stays on your PC and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Notifications, sound and power

The Azan alerts are created on your PC by the app while it runs in the system tray; it starts with Windows (you can turn this off). No server is involved. The Azan sounds are inside the app, so no internet is used for audio. If you turn on "Keep the PC awake", the app asks Windows not to go to sleep while it runs. Nothing is sent to us.

Screensaver mode

The screensaver is a full-screen mode inside the app (not a Windows screensaver file). While it is showing, the app checks for keyboard and mouse activity only to know when to close it. Keystrokes and mouse positions are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

Internet and location (optional). The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone or personal files.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

No personal data is stored on our servers. To remove everything, uninstall the app (Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → SiraatOne Azan → Uninstall).

This website

Website section last updated: 27 November 2026

siraatone.com has no accounts, cookies or ads. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics (see Visitor statistics below). It remembers your reading settings, last position, bookmarks, highlights and notes only in your own browser (never sent to us). A backup file you export is saved only where you choose. When you press play, recitation audio streams from the Quran Foundation recitation sources (verses.quran.foundation, audio.qurancdn.com, mirrors.quranicaudio.com, download.quranicaudio.com), which receives your IP address like any web request. If you choose a full-surah reciter, the audio streams from MP3Quran.net in the same way. When you open a tafsir, it loads from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net, the spa5k/tafsir_api files). The Hadith page loads hadiths live from HadeethEnc.com in the same way. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, it receives your IP address and basic request information.

Visitor statistics. To know how many people use this website, each page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics (static.cloudflareinsights.com). It uses no cookies and no browser storage and does not follow you across other websites. When a page opens it sends Cloudflare the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device type, and page-load timing; like any web request Cloudflare also receives your IP address, which it uses to show the visitor's country. We see only totals (for example visits per day, popular pages and countries), never who you are.

Support form. If you send a message with the form on the Support page, the details you enter (name, email, optional mobile number and device, app, topic, subject and message) are sent through Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form delivery service, to our support mailbox so we can reply to you. We use them only to answer your message and never sell or share them. Nothing is sent until you press Send. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Prayer Times. Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated in your browser. If you tap "Use my location", your browser asks for permission and the coordinates are kept only in your browser; they are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you search for a city instead, only the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) to find the place. Notifications appear only if you allow them in your browser, and only while the page is open. You can clear the saved location at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Zakat calculator. The amounts you enter on the Zakat page stay only in your browser. To fill in today's gold and silver prices, the page downloads a public price file from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) or, if that fails, from currency-api.pages.dev. The only thing in the request is the currency you chose (for example "usd"); like any web request, these services see your IP address. The prices are kept in your browser for 12 hours. You can type your own prices instead.

My Ibadah and Ramadan Tools. What you mark on these pages (prayers, adhkar, Al-Kahf, fasts, Taraweeh nights, your Quran goal and fasts to make up) is saved only in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
