Common questions
The Azan plays late or not at all

Some devices limit apps in the background. Open Android Settings → Apps → SiraatOne Azan → Battery and choose Unrestricted. Also allow notifications and alarms for the app.

Can I use SiraatOne Azan on a phone?

Yes. It is designed for TV and tablet, but you can tap Open anyway on a phone. Azan alarms and sound work fully.

Why does the Quran app need internet?

Only to download the Quran text, recitations, translations and Hadith you choose.

How do I move my bookmarks and notes to a new phone?

Export a backup file in the app, copy it to the new phone, and import it there.

How do I keep my website bookmarks and notes?

They are saved only in your browser. In the Quran reader open Settings → Behavior → Backup to export them to a file, and import that file on another browser.

Do you collect my data?

No. Read our privacy policy.

Become a Tester

Help us test new versions of SiraatOne apps before they reach everyone on Google Play.

How to join

  1. Email us the Google account (Gmail) you use on Google Play.
  2. We add you to the testers list and send you the test link.
  3. Open the link on your Android device and tap Become a tester.
  4. Install the app from Google Play and keep it installed during testing.

What we ask

  • Use the app normally and tell us about anything that does not work.
  • Send your device model and Android version with any problem.
  • You can leave testing at any time.
