Common questions

The Azan plays late or not at all

Some devices limit apps in the background. Open Android Settings → Apps → SiraatOne Azan → Battery and choose Unrestricted. Also allow notifications and alarms for the app.

Can I use SiraatOne Azan on a phone?

Yes. It is designed for TV and tablet, but you can tap Open anyway on a phone. Azan alarms and sound work fully.

Why does the Quran app need internet?

Only to download the Quran text, recitations, translations and Hadith you choose.

How do I move my bookmarks and notes to a new phone?

Export a backup file in the app, copy it to the new phone, and import it there.

Do you collect my data?

No. Read our privacy policy.
