Become a Tester

Help us test new versions of SiraatOne apps before they reach everyone on Google Play.

How to join

  1. Email us the Google account (Gmail) you use on Google Play.
  2. We add you to the testers list and send you the test link.
  3. Open the link on your Android device and tap Become a tester.
  4. Install the app from Google Play and keep it installed during testing.
Email to join

What we ask

  • Use the app normally and tell us about anything that does not work.
  • Send your device model and Android version with any problem.
  • You can leave testing at any time.

We use your email only to add you to the testers list.
