Credits and sources

Quran text

Arabic text (Uthmani, KFGQPC Hafs and IndoPak scripts), word-by-word meanings, Tajweed text, and juz, hizb and page divisions: Quran Foundation (api.quran.com). Saved once and checked: 114 suras, 6236 ayahs.

Translations

All translations come from the Quran Foundation API and are shown unchanged, with the translator's name. Each translation remains the property of its translator or publisher; the default English translation is Saheeh International (Dar Abul-Qasim). They are shown here for free, non-commercial reading only.

Recitations

Audio streams directly from the Quran Foundation recitation servers (verses.quran.foundation, audio.qurancdn.com) and the QuranicAudio mirror of EveryAyah (mirrors.quranicaudio.com). Ayah-by-ayah reciters: Mishary Rashid Alafasy and Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary. Full-surah reciters stream from MP3Quran.net (same list as the SiraatOne Quran app), with each reciter's name shown. Audio is not stored on this website.

Tafsir

Tafsir texts load from spa5k/tafsir_api via jsDelivr (the same source as the app) and are shown unchanged, with the tafsir and author name. Each tafsir remains the property of its author or publisher.

Hadith

Hadiths, translations, grades and explanations are loaded live from HadeethEnc.com (Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths), so you always see their latest version. They are shown without change, following HadeethEnc's republishing terms.

Duas, Adhkar and the 99 Names

From the SiraatOne Quran app's sourced catalogs. The reference for each item (for example Hisn al-Muslim, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim) is shown with that item.

Islamic calendar

Hijri dates are calculated in your browser with the Umm al-Qura calendar. Local moon sighting can differ by a day; you can adjust it on the calendar page.

Fonts

  • IndoPak Nastaleeq and Surah Name V2: from QUL (Tarteel), which publishes no copyright information for them; used unmodified.
