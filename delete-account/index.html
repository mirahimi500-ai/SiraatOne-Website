Delete your SiraatOne account

Signing in to SiraatOne Quran (with Google or Apple) is optional. This page explains how to delete that account and all data synced to it.

In the app (immediately)

  1. Open SiraatOne Quran.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Sync.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all synced data are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email privacy@zaibatech.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". It is done within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and profile photo link.
  • All synced data: bookmarks, favourites, highlights, personal notes, My Ibadah progress and app settings.

Nothing is kept.

Data on your device

Data saved on your device itself can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
